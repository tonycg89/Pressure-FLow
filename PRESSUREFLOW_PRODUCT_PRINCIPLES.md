# PressureFlow Product Principles

## Purpose

This document defines product decision principles for PressureFlow. All future product decisions should protect the beta's core value: helping contractors estimate, sell, schedule, complete, and get paid for exterior cleaning work with less confusion.

## Core Product Focus

- PressureFlow beta is focused on pressure washing and exterior cleaning first.
- Do not expand into new industries until the core workflow is stable.
- Stable workflows take priority over new features.
- Production trust matters more than feature count.

## Contractor-First Experience

- The product should feel fast, simple, and obvious to an owner/operator.
- Reduce clicks where practical.
- Avoid clutter and unnecessary fields.
- Mobile usability is first-class because contractors use the app in the field.
- A contractor should be able to recover from mistakes without losing work.

## Trust and Clarity

- Never show false success.
- Never surprise the contractor with hidden assumptions.
- Customer-facing documents must look professional and be easy to understand.
- Payment, deposit, and balance information must be exact and consistent.

## Data Ownership Principles

- Customer records should not leak jobs, measurements, or history from another customer.
- Address matching should not imply record ownership unless a future property-history feature explicitly supports it.
- Historical records should remain viewable even if old service types are removed from selectable defaults.

## Beta Readiness Principles

Before expanding beta testing:

- Core job workflow must be stable.
- Estimate, contract, invoice, payment, and review flows must work end-to-end.
- Mobile field usage must be reliable.
- Known flow-breaking bugs should be fixed before inviting more testers.

## Feature Discipline

- Do not add new industries, major automation, or advanced reporting while beta-blocking bugs remain.
- Prefer polish, reliability, and workflow speed over feature expansion.
- Any new feature should be evaluated against the question: "Will this help a contractor complete work faster with less confusion?"
